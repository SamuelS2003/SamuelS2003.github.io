My Projects

E-Commerce and Profitability Analysis

I built a four-page Power BI dashboard analysing an e-commerce business's sales, profitability, returns and inventory (€640.42K net sales, 19.42% contribution margin), with each page designed for a specific stakeholder group such as leadership, marketing, operations and inventory planning. It shows that growth is healthy but profit is being eroded by deep-discount campaigns, a €102.90K return loss and €36.71K in sales lost to stock-outs, with Electronics (2.83% margin) the weakest category.

Marketing & Customer Acquisition Analytics

This Power BI analysis examines marketing and customer acquisition across 2,200 customers and €640.42K in net sales, covering channel efficiency, campaign profitability, and customer segment value. It shows that Paid Social absorbs over half of marketing spend for the lowest margin per customer, deep discounts erode margin, and a minority of Loyal, Growth, and VIP customers generate most of the contribution margin.
